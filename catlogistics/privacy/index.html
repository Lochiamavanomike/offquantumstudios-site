English

This Privacy Policy explains how Cat Logistics (the “Game”), developed and published by Off Quantum Studios (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the Game on Android.

1. Summary

  • Cat Logistics does not require a separate Off Quantum Studios account.
  • The Game may use Google and Unity services for advertising, consent management, cloud save, in-app purchases, diagnostics and technical operation.
  • Depending on the features used and your privacy choices, these services may process technical information, identifiers, game progress and advertising-related information.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

The Game stores information locally on your device to provide gameplay and remember your preferences. This may include level progress and current game state, in-game currencies, unlocked and selected themes, gameplay settings, sound and music preferences, language, automatic movement settings, daily reward status, notification/reminder status, advertising-related state and other gameplay preferences.

Some progress may also be synchronized using Google Play Games Services when cloud save functionality is available and enabled.

Clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game may remove locally stored information. Cloud-synchronized data may remain associated with your Google account according to Google's retention and deletion practices.

3. Google Play Games and cloud save

The Game may use Google Play Games Services for player sign-in, achievements and synchronization of progress. These functions use a Google Play Games profile managed by Google; no separate Off Quantum Studios account is created.

When you use Play Games, the Game receives a player identifier and display name from Google. The display name is shown in the Game's settings but is not added to the cloud-save snapshot. For save verification, the Game creates a one-way SHA-256 hash of the Play Games player identifier and includes the hash in the save data. The hash remains an account-related identifier; hashing does not make it necessarily anonymous.

The saved snapshot may include your highest completed level, game currency balances and counters, owned and selected themes, daily reward status, gameplay statistics, a revision value and a random identifier generated for that installation. It does not include your current level layout, gameplay settings or the purchase receipt for “Remove Ads”. The data is transmitted to Google Play Games for saving and restoration; the Game does not operate its own cloud-save server.

We do not receive your Google account password or obtain your Google account email address through this Play Games integration. Google's processing is also governed by its own policies. You can manage or delete Play Games data for individual games through your Play Games profile. Deleting cloud data may affect your saved progress and achievements; it does not automatically erase local data stored on a device.

4. Advertising

Cat Logistics integrates Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising. Formats may include banner ads, interstitial ads and rewarded video ads.

During development and testing, the Game may use Google's test advertising units. Production releases may use real advertising units.

Google Mobile Ads and participating advertising partners may process device and app information, IP address, advertising identifiers including the Android Advertising ID where available and permitted, interactions with advertisements, advertising performance information and other technical identifiers used for ad delivery, fraud prevention, frequency control, measurement and, where permitted, personalized advertising.

Depending on your location, privacy choices and applicable law, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized or otherwise limited.

Cat Logistics does not request access to your device's precise location permission for advertising purposes. Advertising providers may derive or process approximate location-related information, such as from an IP address, according to their own policies and applicable law.

5. Privacy consent and Google UMP

Cat Logistics uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices where required.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other applicable regions, a privacy message may be displayed before advertising services are used.

Depending on the message shown, you may be able to consent, refuse consent, manage individual privacy choices and later review or modify your privacy choices through the privacy options available in the Game.

Your consent status may be stored on your device and processed by Google's consent platform to determine whether advertising requests may be made and what type of advertising may be shown.

Refusing or withdrawing consent does not necessarily remove all advertising. Where legally permitted, non-personalized or limited ads may still be displayed.

6. Advertising ID

The Game includes advertising technology that may access the Android Advertising ID.

The Advertising ID may be used by Google and eligible advertising partners for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, frequency management and personalized advertising where the required consent and legal basis exist.

The availability and use of the Advertising ID depend on Android settings, applicable law and your privacy choices.

7. In-app purchases

Cat Logistics may offer purchases through Google Play, including products such as removing advertisements.

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card number or banking credentials.

The Game may receive purchase-related technical information required to verify and provide a purchase, such as the product purchased, purchase status, transaction or receipt information and whether an entitlement such as “Remove Ads” is active.

8. Diagnostic and technical data through Unity

The Game is developed with the Unity engine. Some versions distributed for testing, including version 0.9.1 (14), include Unity Engine Diagnostics. In versions where it is enabled, Unity may automatically collect crash reports, error and performance information, technical device characteristics and an installation-specific identifier. This collection is separate from advertising consent and is not used by us for advertising.

Off Quantum Studios is responsible for determining the purposes and legal basis of this diagnostic processing; Unity Technologies processes the diagnostic data on our behalf. Unity documents a default retention period of 90 days for this Diagnostics service. Requests concerning data under our responsibility may be sent to support@offquantumstudios.com and may require cooperation with Unity.

Engine Diagnostics has been disabled in the development configuration for upcoming Android builds; this does not retroactively stop collection by earlier versions still installed or erase data already collected. Other providers, including Google Mobile Ads, may still process technical and diagnostic information as described above. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

9. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Cat Logistics may schedule local reminder notifications directly on your device. These reminders do not require a remote push-notification service operated by Off Quantum Studios. You can disable notifications at any time in Android settings.

10. External links and sharing

The Game may open external applications or websites, including Google Play, legal pages, support email applications or Android's sharing interface.

If you use the Share feature, Cat Logistics opens the Android sharing interface. The destination application you choose is responsible for any information you decide to share through it.

If you choose to email our support address, we receive the email address and any other information you voluntarily include in that message. This communication takes place through your email application, not through a separate account-registration or email-collection feature within the Game.

11. Third-party service providers

Depending on the features used, Cat Logistics relies on Google Play, Google Play Games Services, Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform and Google Play In-App Updates. The Game is built with Unity. Unity's Engine Diagnostics applies only to versions where that feature is enabled, as explained in section 8.

Advertising partners identified in the privacy consent message may also process information in connection with advertising. Cat Logistics does not integrate Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.

In-app updates: Google Play In-App Updates may check for a newer version using technical details such as the installed app version. This process is provided by Google Play; Off Quantum Studios does not receive a separate record of these update checks.

12. Sharing and sale of personal data

We do not sell personal data. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate features of the Game, including advertising, purchases, cloud save, consent management and diagnostics.

Advertising data may be processed by Google and advertising partners according to your privacy choices and applicable law.

13. Security

Network communications made by the integrated services (including Google Mobile Ads, Google UMP, Google Play Games, Google Play and Unity) use encrypted connections, such as HTTPS/TLS, according to the providers' implementation and documentation. The Game does not upload personal data to servers operated by Off Quantum Studios.

We rely on the safeguards of the services we integrate. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Retention and deletion

Removing data yourself: You do not need to create or delete an Off Quantum Studios account. To remove local game data, open Android Settings → Apps → Cat Logistics → Storage → Clear data (menu names may vary), or uninstall the Game. To remove data for Cat Logistics from Google Play Games, open your Play Games profile → Your data → Delete Play Games account and data → Delete individual game data → Cat Logistics → Delete. These are Google's own controls; you do not need to delete your entire Google account. See Google's official instructions.

Removing Play Games data may delete cloud progress and achievements, but it does not necessarily delete local game data, Google Play purchase records, advertising provider data or data previously processed by other service providers. If you keep playing with local data present, the Game may synchronize progress again.

  • Local game data: generally remains on your device until changed, cleared through Android settings or the Game is uninstalled. Uninstalling does not necessarily delete cloud data.
  • Cloud save and Play Games: remains subject to Google's account controls and retention practices. You can manage or delete data associated with the Game in your Play Games profile. Deletion may affect achievements or game progress, and it does not automatically clear data kept locally on other devices.
  • Advertising and consent information: retention is determined by Google and, where applicable, advertising partners according to their policies and legal obligations.
  • Purchase information: may be retained by Google Play for transactions, fraud prevention, accounting and other legal obligations.
  • Unity Diagnostics in versions where enabled: Unity documents a default retention period of 90 days. Turning the feature off in a later Game build does not immediately delete earlier records.
  • Support correspondence: messages you voluntarily send may be retained as needed to respond to your request and comply with relevant obligations; no fixed retention period is asserted here.

For questions or requests to exercise applicable privacy rights, including erasure, contact support@offquantumstudios.com. We will review requests concerning data for which we are responsible. Deletion of data controlled independently by Google or other providers may also require using their account tools or making a request to them. We cannot guarantee deletion of all records where legal exceptions apply.

15. Children

Cat Logistics is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not ask users to directly provide personal details such as their name, telephone number, postal address or personal photographs through the Game.

Google Play, advertising and consent services may apply additional protections depending on age, region and the audience configuration of the Game.

16. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to information, access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability or objection, and to withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Where available in the Game, advertising privacy choices can be reviewed and changed under Settings → Privacy options. Some processing by independent service providers is governed by their own privacy controls and policies.

You may contact us at support@offquantumstudios.com about rights relating to data for which we are responsible. If you are in the EEA, you also have the right to complain to a competent data protection supervisory authority.

17. International processing

Google, Unity and advertising partners may process data in countries other than your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area. Where applicable, we and the relevant providers rely on the legal transfer mechanisms and safeguards required by data protection law, as further described in those providers' privacy information.

18. Legal bases for processing

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, the legal basis depends on the specific processing: providing requested game services, cloud synchronization and purchase-related features may be necessary to perform a contract (Article 6(1)(b)); processing for which consent is required, particularly certain advertising purposes, relies on consent (Article 6(1)(a)); processing required by law may rely on legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

Security, fraud prevention and maintaining the reliability of the Game may, where permitted, rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), subject to a balancing assessment and any separate requirements for storing or accessing information on a device. These descriptions do not remove consent requirements imposed by applicable privacy or electronic communications law. Diagnostic processing in older test builds remains subject to the same assessment.

19. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Cat Logistics changes, new functionality is introduced, service providers change or legal requirements are updated. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.

Questa Informativa sulla Privacy descrive come Cat Logistics (“Gioco”), sviluppato e pubblicato da Off Quantum Studios (“noi”), tratta le informazioni quando utilizzi il Gioco su Android.

  • Non vendiamo i tuoi dati personali.

2. Dati salvati sul dispositivo

Il Gioco salva localmente sul dispositivo le informazioni necessarie al funzionamento e per ricordare le tue preferenze. Possono comprendere livello raggiunto e stato della partita, valute di gioco, temi sbloccati e selezionati, impostazioni di gioco, preferenze audio e musica, lingua, movimento automatico, stato delle ricompense giornaliere, stato delle notifiche e dei promemoria, stato relativo alla pubblicità e altre preferenze di gioco.

Alcuni progressi possono inoltre essere sincronizzati tramite Google Play Games Services quando la funzione di salvataggio cloud è disponibile e attiva.

La cancellazione dei dati dell'app o la disinstallazione del Gioco può eliminare i dati memorizzati localmente. I dati sincronizzati nel cloud possono rimanere associati al tuo account Google secondo le politiche di conservazione e cancellazione di Google.

3. Google Play Games e salvataggio cloud

Il Gioco può utilizzare Google Play Games Services per l'accesso del giocatore, gli obiettivi e la sincronizzazione dei progressi. Queste funzioni utilizzano il profilo Google Play Games gestito da Google; non viene creato un account separato Off Quantum Studios.

Quando utilizzi Play Games, il Gioco riceve da Google un identificatore del giocatore e un nome visualizzato. Il nome viene mostrato nelle impostazioni del Gioco, ma non viene inserito nel salvataggio cloud. Per verificare il salvataggio, il Gioco calcola un hash unidirezionale SHA-256 dell'identificatore Play Games e lo include nei dati salvati. L'hash rimane un identificatore collegato all'account: non è necessariamente un dato anonimo.

Il salvataggio può contenere il livello massimo completato, saldi e contatori delle valute di gioco, temi posseduti e selezionati, stato delle ricompense giornaliere, statistiche di gioco, un valore di revisione e un identificatore casuale generato per l'installazione. Non contiene la disposizione del livello corrente, le impostazioni di gioco o la ricevuta dell'acquisto “Rimuovi pubblicità”. Queste informazioni vengono trasmesse a Google Play Games per il salvataggio e il ripristino; il Gioco non utilizza un proprio server per i salvataggi cloud.

Non riceviamo la password del tuo account Google e non otteniamo il tuo indirizzo email Google tramite questa integrazione Play Games. Il trattamento effettuato da Google è disciplinato anche dalle sue informative. Puoi gestire o eliminare i dati Play Games di singoli giochi attraverso il tuo profilo Play Games. L'eliminazione dei dati cloud può comportare la perdita di progressi e obiettivi e non cancella automaticamente i dati locali presenti sul dispositivo.

La revoca o il rifiuto del consenso non significa necessariamente che tutte le pubblicità vengano eliminate. Dove consentito dalla legge, possono essere mostrati annunci non personalizzati o limitati.
